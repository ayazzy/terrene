# Wetland v0.1 real-data iteration report

**CRC can now support serious inspection of real parcel terrain, mapped hydrology and land cover. It still cannot produce a defensible overall restoration ranking for the 12 tested locations.** Required coverage improved materially without weakening gates or inventing measurements.

1. **Implemented:** bounded real source acquisition, native DEM processing, canonical mapping, private immutable evidence storage and job replay, conservative soil-category support, inspectable component categories, separate coverage/quality/unvalidated-model semantics, and saved API/worker/report integration. Existing architecture and historical results remain.
2. **Real sources:** EA 1m composite DTM; OS Open Rivers (official April 2026 GeoPackage); ESA WorldCover 2021 v200; retained EA flood zones and Natural England inventories. EA WWNP reconnection geometry is used independently for comparison only. Soil is not falsely listed as connected.
3. **Populated variables:** elevation mean/min/max/SD; slope mean/p90/max; mapped watercourse distance/intersection/100m adjacency fraction; mapped dominant land cover, built-up fraction, herbaceous-wetland fraction and surface-water fraction; existing mapped flood/protection evidence. Exact canonical names and lineage are in [Nature Intelligence](NATURE-INTELLIGENCE.md).
4. **Remaining required gaps:** soil drainage; actual wetland extent and condition; complete road/rail/infrastructure footprint and irreplaceable-habitat evidence. Generic mapped grassland cannot establish improved/degraded management. Optional relevant-habitat proximity, groundwater and reliable water supply also remain unassessed.
5. **CRC calculations:** actual elevation statistics and Horn 3×3 slope in degrees from 1m elevations; metric nearest-line distance/intersection/buffer fraction; geodesically weighted native land-cover pixel proportions. No full-catchment flow accumulation, D8 routing or TWI is claimed from a bounded parcel DEM.
6. **Real results:** six actual floodplain SSSI polygons have VERY_HIGH physical components on 70% evidence coverage. Wytham's hillside is LOW; urban comparisons vary. Osney Mead is HIGH physically but 99.3% mapped built-up. All overall rankings remain INSUFFICIENT_DATA. See [all 12 results and disagreements](REAL-VERIFICATION.md).
7. **Before/after:** every site's required-input count is **1/10 → 5/10**. Port Meadow's legacy combined support improves **3.3/100 → 20.3/100**, which is not an accuracy or coverage percentage. Port Meadow has mean elevation 57.53m, mean slope 1.18°, full valid DEM cell coverage, watercourse intersection and 23.37% mapped 100m adjacency.
8. **Benchmark:** 294 actual EA WWNP features, complete count/CRS/checksum verified; seven sites overlap and five do not. Missing CRC evidence prevents full ranking comparison. EA reconnection absence is not low wetland potential; incomplete physical components are not asserted to disprove EA mapping. All discrepancies are recorded; no fitting to examples occurred.
9. **Verification:** **98 tests pass on SQLite and 98 on PostgreSQL/PostGIS**; TypeScript, targeted model-panel lint and production build pass. The saved Port Meadow run completed through the actual frontend/API/worker, reproduced deterministically, and exported a complete report; all earlier result hashes stayed unchanged. No browser visual test is claimed. Existing deprecation/chunk warnings remain. Hosted services are still unconfigured; local API/worker/frontend are operational.
10. **Scientific limits:** uncalibrated ordinal/weighted screening, dated/generalized maps, composite survey ages, source omissions, pixel-centre boundary approximation, parcel means hiding local features, missing habitat condition/water supply and incomplete intervention constraints. No validated prediction, statistical interval, hydraulic simulation or restoration recommendation is implied.
11. **Single next priority:** obtain an authorized Oxfordshire LandIS drainage/wetness extract and map its categories conservatively with explicit map-scale uncertainty. The user has no current account/access and requested this gap remain documented. This closes the major physical-input gap; habitat and infrastructure evidence will still be required before an overall ranking is justified.
